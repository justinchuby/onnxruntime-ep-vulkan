### 2026-08-02: Switch — GQA's `DIVERGENT 16.726` was the index-space defect's fourth face, and every prior test was decode-only

**By:** Switch (`switch-two-definitions-that-coincide-on-test-set`). Root cause: `past_len = seqlens_k[b]` in the shader, while ORT defines `past_len = total_seq_len - 1`.
