### 2026-08-02: “Six declines” is a correct self-tally but not a measure of register restraint
**By:** Fact Checker
**What:** Advisory audit does not fully clear the policy claim. Six self-counted decline points can
be reconstructed, but the counting rule is not stable: “coverage does not compose” was explicitly
declined and deliberately left unnumbered while the tally remained four. Several counted declines
also add binding sub-rules, amendments, tokens, or claim semantics. The register avoided a new
top-level `R14`; it did not avoid normative growth.
**Why:** The defensible principle is remedy identity: do not mint two top-level names for the same
falsifying instrument. The unsupported inference is that withholding a top-level number removes the
cost of another rule. Universal, cited, load-bearing obligations still have that cost when nested in
R9/R12/R13 prose. Settlement is a mechanically generated index of every normative obligation,
including amendments, generalisations, drafting rules, and named specimens, plus review by someone
other than the architecture owner before an obligation becomes binding.

## Verification

1. ✅ **Anti-correlated confidence:** declined as `R14`, entered as **R9 rule 5**. Honest
   classification by remedy; not restraint from rule growth, because a numbered sub-rule was added.
2. ✅ **Defaulting lookup / phantom key:** declined as `R14`, entered as **R13 amendment 1**. Again,
   honest top-level deduplication, but a new generally binding obligation was added.
3. ⚠️ **Classifier attracts false instances:** entered under R13 with “quote each instance's own
   evidence.” The remedy is genuinely R13's, but the newly universal human-classifier scope is a
   rule in operational effect even without a number.
4. ✅ **Witness does not discharge a conjunction:** criterion 12 was enumerated under existing R11
   obligations. This is the cleanest honest decline; no distinct remedy or new general mechanism
   was introduced.
5. ⚠️ **A reading that degrades when the subject is fine cannot acquit:** entered as R9's dual. The
   remedy remains a different instrument, but “the reading must be a function of the claim and
   nothing else” is a new universal diagnostic test and is already load-bearing.
6. ❌ **Proof on a device / `PROVEN-ELSEWHERE`:** the R12 diagnosis is sound, but the ruling adds a
   new claimable state, promotion path, disclosure obligations, and a drafting rule. Calling that
   “no mint” describes numbering only; it is architecture-of-record growth.

## Count

✅ Six self-declared tally transitions exist. ❌ Six is not the count of deliberate refusals to
number new content. The criterion-10 “coverage does not compose” section explicitly says
“DELIBERATELY NOT NUMBERED,” adds “a record with two gates owes two extents,” and nevertheless
leaves the tally at four. The sixth ruling also contains a separately binding drafting rule:
prohibitions must be expressed as counted state invariants. Therefore the count is curator-defined,
not reproducible from the register using a stated predicate.

## Loss and re-derivation

⚠️ No later defect was lost because a principle was unnumbered: Morpheus placed the content in
`DESIGN.md`, and later rulings cite it. But principles were re-derived: R9's anti-correlation
analysis reappears as the “dual,” and coverage composition is load-bearing again when an expensive
per-form proof is composed with a cheap model-level port invariant. This is evidence of a
navigability cost, not evidence that the earlier content vanished.

## `PROVEN-ELSEWHERE`

✅ The premise is verified at `d28a04a`: `LedgerEntry.device` exists, while the claim path reduces
`lookup_key` to `LedgerLookup::Hit`; the running device does not participate. Naming that silent
cross-device extrapolation is strictly stronger than today's undifferentiated `PROVEN`.

⚠️ The proposed safety property is unverified because `PROVEN-ELSEWHERE` is not implemented at
`d28a04a`: there is no predicate, counter, disclosure field, or planted control for the token.
“Counted and disclosed” is prose until an artifact proves it. A claimable warning state can become
the new default nobody reads exactly as `device` did.

❌ The cheap-port argument is too broad as written. Trinity independently measured the ULP series
on both devices, so it is no longer supported only by Morpheus's same-day ruling. But that series is
a model-output composite. It may promote only the exact proof keys attributed as executed in that
device run; it cannot promote every ledger entry merely because one model's 65 outputs agree. The
expensive proof and cheap invariant otherwise have different extents and compose to the weaker
extent—the criterion-10 defect again.

## Settlement

- Add a first-class `LedgerLookup` state for same-device versus elsewhere-device evidence.
- Publish counts **and exact proof keys** for all three states in every run.
- Plant a mismatched-device ledger entry and require the lane to emit `PROVEN-ELSEWHERE`.
- Require promotion evidence to carry running device, exact exercised proof keys, attribution, and
  the ULP artifact; unexercised keys remain `PROVEN-ELSEWHERE`.
- Add a reviewer other than Morpheus for normative register additions, regardless of whether they
  receive a top-level number.
